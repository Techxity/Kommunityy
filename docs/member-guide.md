# Kommunity — Member Guide

Welcome! Your community uses Kommunity to stay organised — dues, elections, announcements, and more, all in one place. This short guide shows you how to use it.

If you have a problem, your community admin is the best first contact. For anything else, email **hello@akenrose.com**.

---

## 1. Setting up your account

There are two ways you'll usually get started:

**You received an email invite.** Click the link in the invite, choose a password (at least 8 characters), and you're in. You can tap the eye icon in the password box to check what you typed.

**Your admin shared a community login link.** Open the link (it looks like `.../l/your-community`), and either sign in if you already have an account, or follow the prompts to set one up.

Keep the community login link handy — it's the easiest way back in next time.

---

## 2. Signing in

1. Open your community's login page (or the general **Sign in** page).
2. Enter your email and password. Use the eye icon to reveal your password if you want to check it.
3. Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password?** and follow the link sent to your email.

---

## 3. Your dashboard

When you sign in, your home screen shows everything relevant to you:

- Your **community name** and a welcome.
- **Dues** — how much you owe, how often, and when your next payment is due, with a **Pay now** button.
- **Elections** — if a vote is running, cast it or review the choices you already made.
- **Latest updates** — announcements from your admin.
- **Executive members** — your community's current leaders and their contact details.
- **Call tree** — if you've been paired with someone to check in on, a link to submit feedback.

On your birthday, you'll also see a special greeting.

---

## 4. Your profile

Open **My profile** to see your details. Click **Edit profile** to update your:
- Name, phone, WhatsApp, date of birth, country, and address.

Your **email can't be changed here** — it's your sign-in identity. If it's wrong, ask your admin to update it.

**Receive SMS from my community** — there's a tick box on your profile. Leave it ticked to get birthday wishes, dues reminders, and announcements by text; untick it to stop texts (you'll still get emails).

You can also **change your password** from this page.

---

## 5. Paying your dues

1. On your dashboard, find the **Dues** card and click **Pay now** (or open **Dues**).
2. You'll see the amount and how many periods you're paying for. Confirm and continue to **Paystack** to pay by card, bank transfer, or the other options shown.
3. After a successful payment you'll get an **emailed receipt**, and your dashboard updates to show you're paid up.

Your **dues history** lists every payment you've made, and your dashboard shows the next period you owe from — so you always know where you stand.

If you fall behind, you may receive a friendly reminder by email or text. You can settle up any time from the same **Pay now** button.

---

## 6. Voting in elections

When your community runs an election:
1. Open **Elections** (or use the election card on your dashboard).
2. For each position, review the candidates and select your choice.
3. Submit your vote. **You get one vote per position, and once cast it's locked** — you can come back to view your choices any time.

---

## 7. Staying up to date

- **Updates** shows announcements your admin has sent. New ones also arrive by email (and text, if your community uses SMS and you haven't opted out).
- Check the **Executive members** section any time you need a leader's contact details.

---

## 8. Call tree check-ins

If your community uses the call tree, you may be paired with another member to check in on. When you have an active pairing, a **Submit feedback** link appears on your dashboard — use it to let your admin know how the check-in went.

---

## 9. If you belong to more than one community

If you're a member of several communities on Kommunity — or you also help run one — use the **workspace switcher** at the top right to move between them. Whatever you're viewing (dues, elections, updates) always relates to the community you've selected.

---

## 10. Quick answers

**I didn't get my invite email.** Check your spam folder, then ask your admin to resend it or share the community login link.

**My dues amount looks wrong.** Amounts are set by your admin — reach out to them.

**I want to stop text messages.** Untick "Receive SMS from my community" on your profile. Emails will still come through.

**I can't sign in.** Use **Forgot password?** to reset it. If your email itself is wrong, only your admin can correct it.

Enjoy staying connected with your community.
