# Kommunity — Community Admin Guide

Welcome to Kommunity, the operating system for running your association, alumni network, estate, cooperative, or fellowship. This guide walks you through everything you can do as a community admin, in the order you'll usually need it.

If you ever get stuck, email **hello@akenrose.com** or call **+234 708 974 1989**.

---

## 1. Getting started

### Create your community
1. Go to the Kommunity website and click **Start free trial** (or **Sign up**).
2. Enter your community's name, choose a plan (or start the free trial), and set your name, email, phone, and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. Tick the box to agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, then click **Create community**.
4. If you chose a paid plan, you'll be taken to Paystack to pay. If you started a trial, you go straight to your admin dashboard — nothing is charged until you choose a plan.

### Sign in later
Use the **Sign in** link on the website, or your community's own branded login page (see below). Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the email link.

---

## 2. Your admin dashboard

After signing in you land on the **Overview**, with your community name, plan/trial status, and quick links. The left sidebar is your menu:

- **Overview** — a snapshot of your community.
- **Member Management** — members, chapters, call tree, birthdays.
- **Funds Management** — dues, donations, payouts, financial report.
- **Elections**, **Broadcasts**, **SMS**, **Subscription**, **Settings**.

Some items only appear (or unlock) depending on your plan. Locked items show a padlock and link to your subscription page.

### Switching between communities
If your account manages more than one community — or you're also a member somewhere — use the **workspace switcher** at the top right to move between them. Everything you do always applies to the community you're currently viewing.

---

## 3. Your members' login page

Under **Settings**, you'll find **your members' login page** — a link unique to your community (for example, `.../l/your-community`). Share this link with members instead of the generic sign-in page. It's branded to your community and works with the account each member sets up. Click **Copy link** to share it on WhatsApp, email, or your notice board.

---

## 4. Adding and managing members

Open **Member Management → Members**.

### Add members
- **One at a time:** click add, and fill in the member's details.
- **Bulk import:** upload a CSV (spreadsheet) of members to add many at once. Match the columns to the fields Kommunity expects, and review before importing.
- **Invite by email:** send members an email invite. Invited members skip the approval queue and become active as soon as they set up their account.

### Approve pending members
When someone joins through your community's public join form, they appear as **Pending**. Open **Members → Pending** to review and approve or reject them. Approved members become active and can sign in.

### Chapters
If your community is organised into chapters or groups, create them under **Chapters** and assign members. Chapters help you filter and organise your directory.

### Custom join fields (Onboarding)
Under **Onboarding**, you can configure which details your join form collects — beyond the basics — so new members give you exactly the information you need.

### Member statuses
Members can be **Pending**, **Active**, **Inactive**, or **Rejected**. Only active members receive broadcasts, appear in counts, and can sign in.

## 5. Dues — collecting and tracking

Open **Funds Management → Dues**.

### Set up your dues plan
1. Click **Dues setup**.
2. Give the plan a name (e.g. "Annual dues"), choose how often it's charged (**monthly**, **quarterly**, or **yearly**), and set the amount. You can set an amount in **₦ (NGN)**, **$ (USD)**, or both — leave a currency blank if you don't accept it.
3. Save. Members can now pay from their own dashboards. Saving a new plan replaces the current one but keeps all past payment history.

### See who has paid — and who hasn't
The **Arrears & defaulters** section is the heart of dues management. It shows:
- **Collection rate**, number of **defaulters**, and **total outstanding**.
- A list of every member who is behind — how many periods, how much they owe (in the currency they pay in), when they last paid, and when they were last reminded.

### Send reminders
- Click **Remind** on any member's row to email (and, if SMS is on, text) that person.
- Click **Remind all** to nudge every defaulter at once.
Members with no email or phone on file are flagged and skipped. Automated reminders also go out on a schedule, and each member is only reminded about once a month so nobody is spammed.

### Payment history
The **Payment history** table lists every dues payment recorded, newest first.

## 6. Donations

Open **Funds Management → Donations** to run fundraising campaigns.
1. Create a campaign with a purpose (e.g. "Borehole project") and an optional target and deadline.
2. Share the campaign's link with members and supporters — anyone can donate through it via Paystack.
3. Track contributions as they come in. Donors receive an emailed receipt automatically.

---

## 7. Getting paid — Payouts and bank details

Open **Funds Management → Payouts**.

### Add your bank account
1. In **Bank details**, start typing your bank's name and pick it from the search list.
2. Enter your 10-digit account number. Kommunity checks the number with Paystack and fills in the **account name** automatically — confirm it matches before saving.
3. Click **Save bank details**.

Once verified bank details are saved, **new NGN dues and donations settle straight to your bank account** at the point of payment (minus the platform fee) — no payout request needed. USD collections use the manual payout flow.

### Request a payout
For any balance held on the platform (USD, or NGN collected before you added a bank account), enter an amount and click **Request payout**. The platform team reviews and approves it, and the money is transferred to your saved account. You can watch each request move through **Requested → Approved → Paid** in the payout history.

---

## 8. Financial report (for your records and AGMs)

Open **Funds Management → Financial Report**.

This is your treasurer's statement. Choose a period (**this month**, **this year**, **last 12 months**, or **all time**) and you'll see:
- A **summary** per currency: gross collected, platform fees, and net to your community, split by dues and donations.
- A **monthly breakdown**.
- **Dues contributions by member** — who paid, how much, and when.
- The **all-time funds position**: total net collected, paid out, and what's currently held.

Use **Export CSV** to download the full transaction list (opens in Excel or Google Sheets), or **Print** to produce a clean copy for meeting minutes.

---

## 9. Elections

Open **Elections** to run a transparent vote (e.g. for a new exco).
1. Start an election cycle for the year.
2. Add the **positions** up for election and the **candidates** for each.
3. Members vote from their own dashboards — one vote per position, locked once cast.
4. Watch results and export them when the election closes.

---

## 10. Broadcasts — messaging everyone at once

Open **Broadcasts** to send a single announcement to all active members.
1. Write a **subject** and **message**.
2. Choose how to send it: **Email**, **SMS**, or **both**. (SMS only appears if you've turned it on — see the next section.)
3. If you pick SMS, you'll see a live estimate of pages and cost before you send.
4. Click **Send broadcast**. Members see it on their dashboards and receive it by the channels you chose.

---

## 11. SMS — reaching members by text

Open **SMS**. Text messages reach members who don't check email, and are prepaid and completely separate from your dues, donations, and subscription.

### Turn it on
1. **Enable SMS**, enter your approved **Sender ID**, and choose your **route** (use **DND** so messages reach members on the Do-Not-Disturb list — recommended).
2. Pick which messages use SMS: **birthday wishes**, **dues reminders**, and/or **broadcasts**. Email still sends regardless — SMS is an added channel.

### Fund your wallet
Use **Top up** to add credit through Paystack. You're credited the full amount you choose (the card fee is added at checkout). Each text is charged at the provider's rate — Kommunity adds no mark-up. Set a **low-balance alert** so you're warned before you run out. The **statement** shows every top-up and message with its cost.

> Before SMS can send, your Sender ID must be approved and the DND route activated by the SMS provider. If a message can't send (e.g. empty wallet or an invalid number), Kommunity quietly falls back to email.

---

## 12. Birthdays and Call Tree

**Birthdays** (under Member Management) sends automatic birthday greetings to members on their day, and a daily "celebrants today" digest to you. You can customise the message per month. Greetings go out by email, and by SMS if you've enabled the birthday channel.

**Call Tree** helps you organise member-to-member check-in chains, pair members, and collect feedback that they submit through a link.

---

## 13. Subscription and billing

Open **Subscription** to see your current plan and what's included, and to upgrade or change plans. You can pay **monthly** or **yearly** — yearly is priced at 10 months (two months free). If you're on a trial, you'll get reminders before it ends; nothing is charged until you choose a plan.

---

## 14. Settings

Open **Settings** to:
- Update your **community name** and **type**.
- Copy your **members' login link**.
- **Change your password**.

---

## 15. A good first-week checklist

1. Set your community name and copy your members' login link (Settings).
2. Add or import your members, and approve anyone pending.
3. Set up your dues plan.
4. Add your bank details so money can settle to you.
5. Share your login link with members and ask them to set up their accounts.
6. (Optional) Turn on SMS and top up if you want to reach members by text.
7. Send a welcome broadcast.

That's it — you're running your community on Kommunity.
