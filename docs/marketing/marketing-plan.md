# Kommunity — Marketing & Communications Plan

**Focus:** Associations & cooperatives · Lagos metro first · Shoestring budget (~₦0–30k/month)
**Prepared:** August 2026

This is a founder-led, community-embedded growth plan. With almost no ad spend, the engine is **direct outreach + white-glove onboarding + referral loops + WhatsApp-native content**, aimed at the one pain associations and cooperatives feel every single month: **chasing dues and proving where the money went.**

---

## 1. The strategy in one paragraph

Lagos is dense with associations and cooperatives — professional bodies, market and trade associations, staff and multipurpose cooperatives, thrift/ajo/esusu groups, town unions, and alumni chapters. Every one of them collects dues, argues about who hasn't paid, and struggles to show members a clean financial picture at the AGM. Kommunity solves exactly that. Because these groups run on WhatsApp and word-of-mouth, and because one person usually belongs to several of them, we win by **onboarding a handful of visible "design-partner" associations for free with hands-on setup, turning them into referrals and case studies, and letting the network do the selling.** No paid ads required to start.

---

## 2. Who we're selling to (ICP)

**Primary buyer / champion:** the **Treasurer / Financial Secretary** — they carry the dues pain and the transparency burden. Secondary champions: the **General Secretary** (records, communication) and the **Chairman/President** (accountability, image).

**Ideal first customers (Lagos):**
- Multipurpose & staff **cooperatives** (formal dues, loans, savings, mandatory transparency to members).
- **Professional associations** and trade bodies (regular dues, AGMs, elections).
- **Market/trade associations** and town unions (levies, dues, large membership).
- **Alumni chapters** and **estate residents' associations** as an adjacent, easy expansion.

**Sweet-spot size:** 30–1,000 members. Big enough that spreadsheets and WhatsApp hurt; small enough to decide fast without procurement.

**Disqualifiers (for now):** groups with no dues, no treasurer, or fewer than ~20 members — the pain isn't sharp enough yet.

---

## 3. The problem we lead with (message hierarchy)

Lead with the sharpest pain, then broaden:

1. **"Who hasn't paid?"** — Treasurers chase dues manually on WhatsApp and lose track. Kommunity shows arrears, a defaulters list, and sends automatic reminders.
2. **"Show me the money."** — At AGMs, members demand accountability. Kommunity produces a clean financial report (collected, fees, net, by member) and receipts for every payment.
3. **"Reach everyone."** — Email gets ignored; Kommunity reaches members by SMS (and email) for dues reminders, announcements, and birthdays.
4. **"Run it like an organisation."** — Elections, member directory, donations/levies, all in one place.

## 4. Positioning & core messaging

**Positioning statement:**
> Kommunity is the dues-and-records backbone for African associations and cooperatives — collect dues, know exactly who owes, and show every naira, without the spreadsheets and WhatsApp chaos.

**Tagline options:**
- "Dues collected. Records clear. Members reached."
- "Run your association like it's run well."
- "Every naira accounted for."

**Value props (in the members' language):**
- **Stop chasing dues.** See who's behind and let the app send the reminders.
- **No more AGM arguments.** One clean report of what came in and where it went.
- **Reach members where they are.** SMS + email, not just a WhatsApp message that scrolls away.
- **Fair, naira pricing.** Start free; pay only as you grow. SMS is pay-as-you-go with no mark-up.

**Proof points to build:** first 3–5 design partners' results — "collected ₦X in the first month," "cut defaulters from Y% to Z%," "prepared the AGM report in 10 minutes."

## 5. Channels (ranked by ROI for a shoestring budget)

### Tier 1 — Free, highest leverage (spend 70% of effort here)
- **Direct founder outreach (WhatsApp + calls):** target 15–20 associations/cooperatives at a time. The Treasurer/Secretary is the entry point. (Scripts in the assets pack.)
- **White-glove onboarding as the wedge:** offer to *set it up for them* — import their member list, configure dues, show the arrears view live. This is the single highest-converting tactic and costs only time.
- **Referral loop:** every admin who refers another association gets a reward (free month or bonus SMS credit). Ask at the moment of delight (first dues collected, first clean report).
- **WhatsApp-native content:** Status updates, one-pager PDFs, short tips shared into relevant groups (with permission), and a broadcast list of prospects.
- **Local Facebook & WhatsApp groups** for landlords/estates, cooperative societies, association executives — provide value, don't spam.

### Tier 2 — Free/near-free, compounding
- **LinkedIn + Instagram + X content:** 3 posts/week on dues collection, cooperative record-keeping, AGM prep, financial transparency. Repurpose one idea across all platforms.
- **Case studies / testimonials:** turn each design partner into a 1-page story and a short video/quote.
- **SEO blog posts** (slow burn): "How to collect association dues in Nigeria," "Cooperative financial records made simple," "Preparing your association's AGM report."
- **Partnerships (₦0):** cooperative apex bodies/federations, accountants and consultants who serve co-ops, estate facility managers, association software resellers. They already have the audience; offer revenue share or free tooling.

### Tier 3 — Tiny paid, only when there's spare budget
- **Boosted posts** (₦5–15k) on your best-performing organic post, geo-targeted to Lagos + interest "cooperative/association."
- **Printed flyers/one-pagers** (₦5–10k) for cooperative offices, market association secretariats, and community noticeboards, with a QR code to sign up.
- **Community radio mention** if a free/cheap slot appears (many Lagos community stations are affordable).

## 6. The funnel & growth loop

**Funnel:**
`Awareness (outreach, content, referrals) → Interest (one-pager, demo, free trial) → Activation (white-glove setup → dues plan live → first payment collected) → Retention (arrears tool + reports + SMS keep them logging in) → Referral (reward + case study)`

**The core growth loop:**
1. Onboard an association, set up dues, help them collect the first month.
2. Treasurer looks good at the next meeting (clean report, fewer defaulters).
3. Members who belong to *other* associations ask "what did you use?"
4. We (and the happy admin) get warm intros → repeat.

**Activation is the metric that matters most.** A signup that never sets up dues is dead weight. The white-glove setup exists to force activation.

## 7. 90-day roadmap

**Weeks 1–2 — Foundations**
- Finalise positioning + assets (this pack). Tidy the landing page around the "who hasn't paid" message and add Terms/Privacy links (done) + testimonials placeholder.
- Set up a simple CRM (a spreadsheet or Notion) and a tracking sheet: association name, contact, stage.
- Build a target list of **20 Lagos associations/cooperatives** with a named Treasurer/Secretary contact.
- Set up basic analytics (signups, trial→paid) and a WhatsApp Business profile.

**Weeks 3–6 — Design partners**
- Personally onboard **5–10 associations** free or discounted, with hands-on setup.
- Collect quotes, screenshots, and one metric each ("collected ₦X in month one").
- Publish the first case study and start the 3x/week content cadence.
- Turn on the referral offer and ask every happy admin for one intro.

**Weeks 7–10 — Content + referrals engine**
- Ship 2 more case studies; run a weekly "dues & transparency tip" series.
- Start partnership conversations (co-op federations, accountants, estate managers).
- Print flyers for 5–10 cooperative secretariats/noticeboards.
- First small boosted post on the best organic piece (if budget allows).

**Weeks 11–13 — Scale what works**
- Double down on the outreach message and channel with the best reply/close rate.
- Formalise 1–2 partnerships with revenue share.
- Aim: **20–30 activated associations**, first paying conversions off trials, a repeatable referral flow.

---

## 9. Metrics to watch (keep it simple)

- **Activated associations** (set up a dues plan + collected ≥1 payment) — north-star.
- **Dues collected through the platform (GMV)** — proves value and drives word-of-mouth.
- **Trial → paid conversion rate.**
- **Referral rate** (intros per happy admin).
- **Reply/close rate** by outreach message (so you can kill what doesn't work).
- **MRR** and churn once paying customers exist.

Review weekly. If activation stalls, the fix is almost always *better onboarding*, not more leads.

---

## 10. Risks & how we handle them

- **"We already use a spreadsheet / WhatsApp."** — Don't argue; show the arrears view and the AGM report on their own data. Seeing beats telling.
- **Trust with money.** — Lead with transparency: receipts, the financial report, and the fact that dues can settle straight to their own bank account via Paystack. Offer to start on a small/pilot basis.
- **SMS deliverability / cost.** — Emphasise pay-as-you-go, no mark-up, and DND-route delivery. It's optional and prepaid.
- **Slow, high-touch onboarding limits volume.** — That's fine early; it buys testimonials and referrals. Systematise (a setup checklist + a short Loom video) once the motion is proven.
- **Founder time is the bottleneck.** — Protect 2–3 focused outreach/onboarding blocks per week; batch content.

---

## 11. What "good" looks like at day 90

- 20–30 associations/cooperatives activated in Lagos.
- 3–5 written/video case studies with real numbers.
- A referral loop producing warm intros weekly.
- A repeatable outreach script with a known reply/close rate.
- First trial→paid conversions and the beginnings of MRR — all on effectively zero ad budget.
